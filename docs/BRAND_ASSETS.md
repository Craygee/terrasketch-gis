# LandDraft brand assets

The official LandDraft identity is the parcel-survey mark: an irregular mapped parcel divided by
survey lines with a central point. It represents land, geometry, field coordinates, and drafting
without borrowing the visual identity of another GIS product.

## Official lockups

- `public/landdraft-logo.svg` — primary horizontal logo with wordmark and descriptor.
- `public/landdraft-mark.svg` — primary square mark for the product interface.
- `public/landdraft-mask.svg` — monochrome mark for Safari pinned tabs and single-color use.
- `public/favicon.ico`, `favicon-16x16.png`, and `favicon-32x32.png` — browser icons.
- `public/apple-touch-icon.png` — iPhone and iPad home-screen icon.
- `public/landdraft-icon-192.png`, `landdraft-icon-512.png`, and
  `landdraft-maskable-512.png` — installable web-app icons.
- `public/landdraft-social-card.png` — 1200×630 link-preview image.

## Core colors

- Field green: `#227448`
- Paper cream: `#FFF9E9`
- Page paper: `#F7F4E9`
- Deep ink: `#173328`
- Supporting gray-green: `#5D6D65`

Keep the mark proportions and line geometry intact. Do not recolor individual survey lines, place
the mark over a busy image, or combine it with another product's symbol. Raster assets can be
regenerated with `scripts/generate-brand-assets.py` when a new size is required.
